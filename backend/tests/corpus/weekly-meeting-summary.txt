Weekly team meeting summary.

The team reviewed progress on the current sprint and agreed that the
ingestion pipeline work is on track, with parsing and chunking mostly
complete and indexing left to finish this week.

Several open questions were raised about how to handle scanned PDFs
that contain little or no extractable text. The group agreed to detect
pages with low text yield automatically and queue them for OCR rather
than failing the whole document.

Action items: write a short design note on the OCR fallback, add a few
scanned test files to the corpus, and schedule a follow-up check once
the retrieval step is working end to end.
