# Hiking Safety Guide

A safe hike starts well before you reach the trailhead, with planning
that matches the route to your fitness level and the day's conditions.

## Before You Go

Check the weather forecast, tell someone your planned route and return
time, and pack more water than you think you will need. Trails that look
easy on a map can take much longer than expected.

## On the Trail

Stay on marked paths, watch your footing on loose rock or roots, and
turn back if weather changes suddenly. If you are hiking in a group,
keep the slowest member in sight rather than letting the group spread
out.

## If Something Goes Wrong

Carry a basic first aid kit and know how to use it. If you get lost,
stop moving, try to retrace your last known position, and use a whistle
or phone to signal for help rather than wandering further off the path.
