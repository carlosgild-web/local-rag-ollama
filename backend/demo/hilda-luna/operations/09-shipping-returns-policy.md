# Hilda & Luna Beadworks — Shipping & Returns Policy

> **[Sample data]** Fictional policy document generated for RAG testing purposes.

**Effective date:** February 1, 2026
**Applies to:** All retail and wholesale orders placed through hildaluna-sample.com or a wholesale account

## 1. Shipping Zones & Carriers

| Zone | Carrier | Typical transit time |
|---|---|---|
| Mexico (domestic) | Estafeta | 2–4 business days |
| United States & Canada | DHL Express | 5–8 business days |
| Rest of Latin America | DHL Express | 6–10 business days |
| Europe (select wholesale only) | FedEx International | 8–12 business days |

## 2. Shipping Costs

- Retail orders under $50: flat $6.50 domestic / $18 international
- Retail orders $50+: free domestic shipping; international shipping discounted 50%
- Wholesale orders: shipping cost calculated at checkout based on weight and destination; orders over $750 ship free domestically

## 3. Processing Times

| Order type | Processing time | Ships via |
|---|---|---|
| Retail / hobbyist | 1–3 business days | Standard carrier |
| Boutique wholesale | 5–7 business days | Standard carrier |
| Large wholesale / distributor | 10–14 business days | Freight or standard, depending on volume |
| Custom color orders | 15–20 business days | Standard carrier, after color approval |

Processing time does not include transit time. Orders placed after 2:00 PM local time are processed the next business day.

## 4. Returns

### 4.1 Eligible for return
- Unused, unopened beads and charms, in original packaging
- Returned within **30 days** of delivery date
- Original receipt or order number required

### 4.2 Not eligible for return
- Custom color orders
- Clearance / Scrap Bin items
- Opened findings (clasps, jump rings, wire) once the packaging seal is broken, for hygiene and quality-control reasons

### 4.3 How to start a return
1. Email `support@hildaluna-sample.com` (sample) with your order number.
2. We issue a prepaid return label for domestic orders within 2 business days.
3. Refunds are processed within 5 business days of receiving the returned item, to the original payment method.

## 5. Damaged or Incorrect Items

- Report damage or incorrect items within **7 days** of delivery, with photos.
- Breakage claims under 20 pieces are replaced **without** requiring a return shipment.
- Breakage claims of 20+ pieces may require the damaged items to be returned for carrier claim purposes; we will send a prepaid label.

## 6. Order Changes & Cancellations

- Orders can be modified or cancelled free of charge **before** they enter processing.
- Once an order shows "Processing" status in the portal, changes are not guaranteed, since picking may have already begun.

## 7. International Customs & Duties

International customers are responsible for any customs duties, import taxes, or brokerage fees assessed by their country. Hilda & Luna Beadworks does not prepay or reimburse these charges.

## 8. Questions

Contact `support@hildaluna-sample.com` (sample) or your wholesale account manager for anything not covered above.
