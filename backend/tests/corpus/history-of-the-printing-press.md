# A Short History of the Printing Press

Before mechanical printing, books were copied by hand, a slow process
that limited how widely knowledge could spread.

## Gutenberg's Invention

Around 1440, Johannes Gutenberg in Mainz developed a printing system
using movable metal type, an oil-based ink, and a modified wine press.
His most famous work, a Latin Bible, demonstrated the method's quality.

## Spread Across Europe

Within decades, printing presses appeared in cities across Europe. The
falling cost of books helped spread literacy and new ideas, including
scientific and religious texts that reached far wider audiences than
before.

## Lasting Impact

Historians often credit the printing press with accelerating the
Renaissance and the Reformation by making it possible to reproduce and
distribute texts quickly and cheaply, forever changing how information
moves through society.
