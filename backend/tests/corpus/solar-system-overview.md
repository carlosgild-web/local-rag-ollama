# The Solar System: An Overview

The solar system consists of the Sun and everything that orbits it,
including eight planets, dozens of moons, and countless smaller bodies
such as asteroids and comets.

## The Inner Planets

Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars are called the inner or terrestrial
planets. They are rocky, relatively small, and orbit closest to the Sun.
Earth is the only one known to support life.

## The Outer Planets

Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune are the outer or gas giant planets.
They are much larger than the inner planets and are made mostly of
hydrogen and helium, with deep atmospheres and many moons.

## Smaller Bodies

Beyond Neptune lies the Kuiper Belt, home to dwarf planets like Pluto.
The asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter contains millions of rocky
fragments left over from the formation of the solar system.
