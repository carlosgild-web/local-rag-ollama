CUSTOMER FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

ORDERING

Q: What is the minimum order for wholesale pricing?
A: Boutique wholesale requires a $150 minimum order; large wholesale/distributor pricing requires
   a $750 minimum order. Retail/hobbyist orders have no minimum.

Q: How long does an order take to ship?
A: Retail orders ship in 1–3 business days. Boutique wholesale orders ship in 5–7 business days.
   Large wholesale/distributor orders ship in 10–14 business days.

Q: Can I get a custom bead color made?
A: Custom color runs are evaluated case by case and generally require a minimum order of 2kg
   of glass per color. Contact wholesale@hildaluna-sample.com (sample) to request a quote.

SHIPPING & RETURNS

Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Yes, to the United States, Canada, and most of Latin America. International orders may be
   subject to customs duties, which are the customer's responsibility.

Q: What is your return policy?
A: Unused, unopened beads and charms may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full
   refund. Custom color orders and clearance/scrap-bin items are final sale.

Q: My order arrived with broken beads. What do I do?
A: Contact support within 7 days of delivery with photos of the damage. We will replace the
   damaged items at no charge; no return shipment is required for breakage claims under 20 pieces.

PRODUCTS

Q: Are your beads and charms nickel-free?
A: All charms are cast in lead-free pewter alloy. Our gold-plate and rhodium finishes are
   nickel-free. Antique bronze finish contains trace nickel; customers with nickel sensitivity
   should choose gold-plate or rhodium finishes instead.

Q: What's the difference between the Coastal Garden and Amber Hollow collections?
A: Coastal Garden (Spring/Summer) features pastel glass beads with botanical and seashell
   charms. Amber Hollow (Fall/Winter) features warm amber, rust, and forest-green tones with
   acorn, moon, and mushroom charms.

Q: Do you sell findings (clasps, jump rings, wire) separately from beads?
A: Yes, findings and components are sold individually and are not included in bead or charm
   catalog pricing unless listed as part of a kit.

ACCOUNT & WHOLESALE

Q: How do I apply for a wholesale account?
A: Submit a wholesale application through the website with your business license or resale
   certificate. Approval typically takes 3–5 business days.

Q: Do you offer a loyalty or rewards program for retail customers?
A: Not currently, but retail customers are notified first about scrap-bin sales and
   limited-release colorways via our email list.
