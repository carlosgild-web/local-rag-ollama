Basic first aid knowledge can make a real difference before professional
help arrives.

For minor cuts, clean the wound with water, apply gentle pressure with a
clean cloth to stop bleeding, and cover it with a sterile bandage. Seek
medical attention if the bleeding does not stop after several minutes of
steady pressure, or if the cut is deep.

For burns, cool the area under running water for several minutes. Do
not apply ice directly to a burn, and avoid breaking any blisters that
form, since this increases the risk of infection.

If someone is choking and cannot speak or cough, stand behind them, lean
them forward, and give firm blows between the shoulder blades. If that
does not clear the blockage, abdominal thrusts may be needed, and
untrained bystanders should call for emergency help immediately.

For a suspected sprain, remember rest, ice, compression, and elevation
of the injured area during the first day to reduce swelling.
