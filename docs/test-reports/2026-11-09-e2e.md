# End-to-end test, 2026-11-09 (v0.6.0, before going public)

**What was tested:** the whole app on the `chore/prepare-public` branch (v0.6.0 plus the security and Docker work), starting from an empty database, with:
- a real Ollama: `qwen2.5:3b` for chat, `bge-m3` for embeddings
- the 11 sample documents in `e2e/corpus/` (fictional Hilda & Luna Beadworks data)
- edited versions, new documents and edge cases
- the restart check
- a separate check of **Luna** through the employee-portal channel

**How:** `e2e/run_e2e.py` with `e2e/scenario.json`, on an isolated server (port 8012, its own data folder). The restart check stopped and restarted that server mid-job. The Docker image was checked by hand the same day ([CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md)).

**Detailed results:** [run report](2026-11-09-e2e-run-report.md), with every question, answer, source and timing.

**Machine:** Ryzen 5 7520U laptop, 7.2 GB RAM, CPU only. The median answer took 58.9 s and the slowest 188 s. The laptop was short of memory throughout, which made answers slower than on 11-08.

## Verdict

**All 53 must-pass checks passed**, the same as the 11-08 run:
- ingestion of every file type
- versions and rollback, and the trash
- duplicates and bad files
- jobs (cancel and retry)
- reindex and blue/green rebuild
- suggested shelves
- the troubleshooter
- crash recovery: the job interrupted by the restart completed, and documents, trash and 55 chat sessions survived

**Grounding holds:** 7 of 7 off-topic, wrong-shelf and missing-fact questions were answered "not found in the documents". The prompt-injection document didn't take over any answer.

**Answer quality: 53 of 57**, one better than 11-08.

## A bug found and fixed on the way

An earlier attempt the same day stopped in the `ui_endpoints` phase. Chroma 1.5.x failed to read a shelf's vectors right after a large upload, with "Error creating hnsw segment reader: Nothing found on disk". The job's three quick retries all landed inside that short window. The data was fine: the same reads worked minutes later.

The Chroma store now retries vector reads on that one error, for up to about 15 seconds; tests cover it (`backend/tests/test_chroma_store.py`). This run didn't hit the window.

Two other attempts were stopped by the machine running out of memory, not by the app. Every app check that ran in those attempts passed too.

## The 4 quality failures

All four are *answer misses*: the right passage was retrieved, but the 3B model didn't state the fact as expected.

| Id | Question | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| hr-02 | What time is the holiday party and where is it held? | Gave the date and the place, but not the time (6:00 PM). |
| hr-04 | What type of gloves must be used when handling resin? | Right answer (nitrile), but it wrote `[N1]` instead of a valid `[1]` citation. |
| sc-06 | Are antique bronze charms nickel-free? | Answered "not found", although the FAQ passage says bronze has trace nickel. |
| sc-07 | How long does approval of a wholesale account application take? | Answered "not found", although the FAQ says 3–5 business days. |

sc-06 and sc-07 were already open after 11-06. Both facts sit in `08-customer-faq.txt`, which is one long file split into two chunks. The small model tends to refuse when the answer is a single line inside a long Q&A chunk. Smaller chunks for FAQ-style documents, or a 7B/8B model on better hardware, are the likely fixes.
